# Goal LEARN-SME-APPOINTMENT-001

Status: PROPOSED - NOT AUTHORIZED

## Objective

Define and fill the qualified human SME/reviewer-of-record role for the Internal
Level II Learning and Readiness Pilot, then establish a version-bound technical
and safety review rubric.

## Human Input Required

The stakeholder must name a qualified SME or approved candidate pool. Agents may
prepare criteria and evidence but cannot manufacture qualifications or final SME
acceptance.

## Allowed

- draft minimum qualification and conflict criteria;
- draft appointment, confidentiality, availability, and review-service terms;
- define the content, calculation, safety, standards-edition, worked-example,
  question-key, and contested-claim rubric;
- define decision dispositions and expiry/re-review triggers; and
- prepare the ET-010..014 review queue.

## Forbidden

- source-body or content authoring;
- recording an agent as the qualified human SME;
- accepting any content version before the SME is appointed; and
- learner-data, import, render, deploy, or release work.

## Acceptance

- named human and authority evidence;
- qualification and conflict assessment;
- accepted review rubric;
- exact version-binding and decision template; and
- escalation and replacement path when the SME is unavailable or conflicted.

## Next Decision

Stakeholder names the SME/candidate pool and authorizes the bounded appointment
packet.
