Prepare version-bound source-admissibility decisions for the 12 registered
ET-010..014 source rows without inspecting or transforming learning source
bodies.

- Rights authority: `LEARN-RIGHTS-2026-07-11`
- Program Manager: `LEARN-PM-2026-07-14`
- Primary executor: to be assigned separately
- Independent evidence auditor: to be assigned separately

## Allowed

- inspect register fields, official rights metadata, access records, hashes, and
  custody references admitted by the goal;
- use the verified Olares read-only mount for admitted path, filename, stat, hash,
  and control-field observations without opening source bodies;
- prepare source-specific decision packets;
- identify missing evidence and replacement-source needs; and
- obtain rights-authority dispositions.

## Forbidden

- opening quarantined or derived learning bodies;
- writing, renaming, deleting, or generating files on the read-only source mount;
- copying, paraphrasing, authoring, or rendering learner content;
- treating the standing policy as source-specific permission;
- changing source classifications without the rights-authority decision; and
- import, database, API, deploy, or release work.

## Acceptance

- every row is bound to an immutable register snapshot or row version;
- lawful-access and license states are explicit;
- allowed and prohibited uses are testable;
- unknown remains conditional, candidate, replacement-required, or prohibited;
  and
- every accepted decision has owner, evidence, conditions, timestamp, expiry,
  and affected target/version.
